# Safety and go-live boundary

The hosted Vercel build serves a synthetic-data demo with browser-local storage, legacy shared-D1 bearer links, and a pseudonymous account-D1 mode. Each shared/account workspace contains only the built-in fictional sample. Legacy bearer links are fixed to Provider or Client, expire after 30 days, and do not authenticate their holders. Account mode issues a random handle and 256-bit account key, stores only its SHA-256 hash, and uses a seven-day Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite=Strict session cookie. Accounts/workspaces expire after 30 days; a Client invitation is hashed, single-use, and bound to the first eligible account that claims it. Server-side membership fixes one Provider and one Client account per workspace. Account keys are not recoverable, but a signed-in account can rotate its key and revoke other sessions. These controls prove possession of a key, not a person's real-world identity, email, or legal authority. Account and shared D1 modes block record creation/deletion and all PayPal routes. In the legacy bearer mode, a Provider-link holder can replace an unused Client token; the Provider link itself cannot be revoked. Selected AI source text is sent over HTTPS to the existing temporary authenticated Ollama bridge where enabled and may be unavailable when its host sleeps; manual entry remains available. Browser-local PayPal uses Sandbox only. JSON/CSV exports are local to the visitor, and JSON exports embed attachments.

The local Node server is a disposable prototype, not a live contract, client portal, legal record, marketplace, or payment service.

1. Keep demo data fictional; do not paste real client agreements, emails, bank details, or cards. The fictional-only confirmation is a user attestation; PACT does not inspect or technically block real personal or contract data. Optional attachments are limited to small PDF, PNG, JPEG, TXT, CSV, or Markdown files. They stay in browser-local storage in that mode and are stored with the single shared fictional record in D1 in shared mode; PACT does not scan or verify them. Only the selected source text is sent to the temporary AI bridge when a visitor explicitly requests AI suggestions.
2. In browser-local mode the Provider/Client role switcher is only a simulator. Legacy shared-D1 roles are fixed by hashed bearer tokens. Account D1 roles are fixed by server-checked memberships, but account keys are anonymous possession credentials, not identity checks; a stolen key/browser can be misused and lost keys cannot be recovered. Account rate limits are in-memory and rely on forwarded client-IP data; they are best-effort, may be bypassed, and are not durable abuse controls. The Provider bearer link itself cannot be revoked. Production requires a reviewed identity/recovery model, revocable invitations, party-scoped authorization on every operation, CSRF/session controls, durable abuse controls, backups, managed retention/deletion, monitoring, and independent security review.
3. PayPal credentials must be Sandbox-only. Every standard Orders v2 capture goes to the configured Sandbox merchant account. No provider payout, split settlement, or escrow is implemented. PayPal's production marketplace services require partner approval.
4. The local JSON file store is single-process and not production persistence. Browser-local demo data stays in each browser. Shared D1 stores one fictional sample per workspace, caps active workspaces/accounts at 128 and workspaces at three per account, expires account/session/workspace data after 30/7/30 days, and cleans expired rows when a new share, account, or workspace is created. These limits and in-memory request throttles are demo guardrails, not production abuse protection or a durable identity service.
5. Optional browser notifications are opt-in, local to one browser, and run only while the app is open. They are not a durable reminder service, email, push notification, or background scheduler.
6. Before any real-user or live-payment use, complete privacy, legal, security, accessibility, reliability, payment-partner, and operational reviews. Never imply that a payment proves delivery or creates escrow.

The browser-local PayPal integration is available for judges to inspect, but it is not authenticated: the role selector is a simulator, not an access-control boundary. It is Sandbox-only, rate-limited in memory, limited to USD, and capped by the prototype's amount validation. Both shared-link and account D1 modes disable PayPal entirely. Use synthetic data and your own Sandbox buyer; no buyer login is provided. Never use saved buyer credentials to approve additional orders without a new, exact order authorization.
